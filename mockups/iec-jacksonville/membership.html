Membership

Two ways in. One is for the people who hold the license.

Founding membership is open now. Firms that join before the charter is filed help set the dues structure, the class schedule and the first year’s priorities — and are listed as founding members [permanently / for the first five years].

Licensed electrical contractors

Contractor Member

[$X,XXX / year]

[Dues scale by number of field employees — structure TBD]

For firms holding a Florida electrical contractor license and doing work in Duval, Clay, Nassau or St. Johns. This is the voting membership — contractor members elect the board and are the only members who can sponsor apprentices.

What you get

  • Sponsor apprentices into the four-year program
  • A vote on the board, the budget and the dues structure
  • Member tuition rates for apprenticeship and continuing education
  • Monthly membership meetings — the room where the market talks
  • Listing in the chapter’s find-a-contractor directory
  • Chapter representation on code, licensing and workforce issues
  • Access to national IEC member programs — [safety, HR and insurance programs TBD]

Joining

Three steps, about two weeks

  1. 1

    Request the packet

    Tell us your firm name, license number and rough field headcount. We send dues, bylaws and the sponsorship agreement.

  2. 2

    Board review

    Applications are reviewed at the next board meeting — [monthly, second Tuesday]. Contractor applicants must hold an active Florida license in good standing.

  3. 3

    Welcome and onboarding

    Dues are invoiced on approval. Your firm is added to the directory, and if you plan to sponsor an apprentice, the training director schedules an intake call.
