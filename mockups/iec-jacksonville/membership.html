Membership

Membership is how the chapter exists.

There is no chapter without member contractors and industry partners — they fund the school, seat the board and set the calendar. Charter membership is open now.

Why join

Association membership is leverage you can’t buy alone.

IEC Jacksonville is an association of electrical, low-voltage and renewable-energy contractors, plus the distributors, manufacturers and professional firms that serve them. Two membership classes, one chapter.

The pitch is simple. A single contractor cannot run an accredited apprenticeship, negotiate national benefit programs, or get a licensing board to return a call. [N] contractors together can do all three.

Charter members

Companies that join before the chartering meeting are founding members: they vote on the first slate of officers, the dues schedule and the training calendar, and they are listed as founders permanently.

Contractor Membership

For licensed electrical, low-voltage and renewable-energy contractors doing business in Northeast Florida. This is the voting membership — contractors run the chapter.

What you get

  • Sponsor apprentices into the four-year program at member rates
  • Send licensed staff to code update, safety and CEU sessions
  • A seat and a vote on chapter direction, dues and the training calendar
  • Eligibility to run for the board and the training committee
  • Access to IEC National member benefit programs — insurance, safety, business tools
  • Listing in the chapter’s contractor directory
  • Monthly membership meetings and contractor-only roundtables
  • Job-board access when apprentices and journeymen are looking

Class B

Industry Partner Membership

For the distributors, manufacturers, reps and professional service firms that sell to and support electrical contractors. Non-voting, three tiers.
